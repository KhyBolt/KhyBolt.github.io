Aircraft Geolocation with Audio

I built this to see if the sound of passing aircraft could help locate a recording. Given a rough time, a search area, and two audible flyovers, it compares pitch changes with archived flight tracks and ranks possible recording locations.

The replay method, archive query, and score metadata document the inputs and fit scores. The archived inputs can be checked against their digests. Repeating the search requires solver revisions that are unavailable through the public links.

The study overlays use the published recorder coordinates and matching KML samples during each recording. The public replay bundle contains no fitted point, scored track geometry, or finer grid scores.
