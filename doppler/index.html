Aircraft Geolocation with Audio

I built this to figure out where an audio or video clip was recorded. If it captures two audible aircraft flyovers, the tool compares how their pitch changes with historical flight tracks to estimate where the recorder was.

Colsto’s YouTube video gave me the idea. This is my own implementation.

Livermore example case

California · 17 May 2026

For this run, I used two pitch traces and their matching flight tracks from a published study. The best fit was 144 m from the published phone location. It’s an estimate, not a confirmed fix.
