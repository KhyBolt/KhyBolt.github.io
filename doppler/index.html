Aircraft Geolocation with Audio

I built a Python tool that uses the changing pitch of two flyovers and historical flight tracks to estimate where a recording was made.

Aircraft Doppler on GitHub

Colsto’s YouTube video gave me the idea. This is my own implementation.

Two flyovers in Livermore

California · 17 May 2026

I pulled the pitch tracks straight from two phone recordings, 17 minutes apart, and searched a full day of flight data without giving the tool the flight IDs.

It ranked the right pair, N9393T → N86LP, first out of 12,215 pairs with track coverage. The best fit was about 405 m from the study’s phone location.
