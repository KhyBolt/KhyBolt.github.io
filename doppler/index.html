03 / The evidence

How the search worked.
  1. Find two sustained falling tones in the recording, before looking at the flight tracks.
  2. Use candidate flight tracks and sound travel time to predict pitch at each receiver position, allowing one shared offset between the audio and track clocks.
  3. Refine the best-fitting areas. Return a point only when the search passes the benchmark’s checks.

The checks are not a guarantee. A deliberately constructed pair of tracks with indistinguishable Doppler curves still produced a wrong result. The 14.2 m error above applies only to this fixture.

All audio and tracks are simulated. This page replays a saved run; it doesn’t process other recordings or look up aircraft.
